What is this app?

D&D Monster Manager is a server-rendered web application for browsing and managing Dungeons & Dragons monsters with strict role-based access control.

  • ADMIN — Full access to all monsters. Can create, edit, and delete both official and community entries.
  • DM — Can view official monsters and create community monsters for personal use.
  • PLAYER — Read-only access to official monsters.
